The Studio

Spaces that tell a story and truly reflect the people who inhabit them

Nteriors is a London-based interior design studio founded in 2023 by Nali Malungu. Trained as an interior designer with a background in project management, Nali brings both creativity and structure to every project.

Drawing inspiration from her expressive Congolese heritage, Nteriors explores colour, form, texture, and materials to craft interiors that feel layered, soulful, and full of character. Grounded in faith and guided by harmony, each space is as functional as it is beautiful.

What We Do

A full-service studio, from concept to completion

Every project is approached with intention. We combine creative direction with rigorous project management to deliver spaces that are both beautiful and functional.

01 Discovery & Consultation

We begin with an initial consultation to understand your brief, vision, lifestyle, needs, and the purpose of the space.

02 Concept Development

We create the creative direction for your project, including moodboards, colour palettes, material inspiration, and overall design narrative.

03 Space Planning & Layout

Optimising flow, functionality, and comfort to ensure the space supports everyday living.

04 Design Development

We refine concepts with detailed selections, drawings and renders covering furniture, finishes, lighting, textures, and key design elements.

05 Project Coordination

Structured support for sourcing, working alongside suppliers and trades to ensure the design is executed smoothly.

06 Styling & Final Finishing

Curating furnishings, decor, and layered details that bring the space together and complete the vision.
